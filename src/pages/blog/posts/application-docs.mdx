## Steps to Seek NOC for Internship

### 1. Undertaking
- Write an undertaking stating you'll maintain 75% attendance.
- Submit it to the Department Office for the HOD's signature.
- Forward the signed undertaking to the Academic Section.

### 2. NOC Format
- Request the AR Academic to issue the NOC format with internship details.
- Apply for leave via the leave portal or through the department office if your ID is inactive.

### 3. Leave and NOC Documents
- Collect a signed leave printout from the Department Office (signed by FA and HOD).
- Obtain a pre-written application from the B.Tech section in the Academic Section and fill it out.
- Submit the NOC, application, and leave printout for the AR Academic's signature.
- Submit all documents to the Academic Section door entry officer for photocopying. The original will be returned to you.

## Degree and Transcript-Related Queries

### 1. Missing/Wrong Grade in Transcript
- Mail AR Academic at [aracademic@iitp.ac.in](mailto:aracademic@iitp.ac.in), cc acadbtech and skverma@iitp.ac.in.
- If unresolved, inform the Academic Secretary or UGR.

### 2. Degree Certificate After Graduation
- Fill out the [Degree Certificate Form](https://academics.iitp.ac.in/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=78&Itemid=265).
- Mail the form with the receipt to acadbtech, cc aracademic, dracademic, and UGR.

### 3. Transcripts for First Four Semesters (21 Batch)
- Pay ₹100 per person at IIT Bihta Bank (details available in the Academic Office).
- Submit a declaration stating no further transcript requests.
- Submit documents to AcadBTech for attested transcripts.

### 4. CPI to Percentage Conversion
- [CPI to Percentage Conversion Formula](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1G2Dzi5O8TMA3FKJctGnyRQWZXkLlNvBT/view?usp=sharing)

### 5. Proof of English as Medium of Instruction
- Submit a written application to the Associate Dean Academic UG.

### 6. Interim Transcripts
- [Interim Transcripts Link](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BnBAZRyFJj8iJq3Ko7lGK9Z2EU_VrMfV/view?usp=drive_link)

### 7. Final Consolidated Transcripts
- Provided during convocation.

## 6-Month Internship NOC

### 1. Documents to Prepare
- Application (use the template: [6-Month Internship Application](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1qUtMmTB3ngs6qCeO1xRldE2QrmquVRZ1ZzB45_EC7_E/edit?tab=t.0)).
- Include the number of courses based on the semester structure.
- [Office order](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1eUy2XAaaCdr-xAntBDUOjlfuElJsyH_V/view?usp=sharing) and proof of internship offer.
- Application signed by HOD requesting online recordings for the third course (non-NPTEL mapped).

### 2. Submission Process
- Go to the Admin Building (1st floor, Associate Dean Academic UG office) and submit the documents.
- The Associate Dean will verify the documents and forward them to the Academic Section for approval.

### 3. Receiving NOC
- You will receive the NOC from the Academic Section (it’s better to bring a template with you).
